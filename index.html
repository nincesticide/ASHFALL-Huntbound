Cinder’s Wager

A camp gambling game played only with ASHFALL in-game gold. Build a hunt closest to the Bounty without overreaching.
How Cinder’s Wager Works
1. Read the Bounty. This is the target Glory number you want to finish closest to.
2. Read your two cards. Their values plus any pattern bonus create your current Glory.
3. Choose exactly one finish: Hold Two keeps your hand, Bind Omen adds the visible shared Omen card, or Draw Blind adds one unknown card.

Win condition: finish closer to the Bounty than the House. Going over is allowed—the game compares absolute distance. Ties go to the House. Hitting the Bounty exactly pays the best reward.
Example: Bounty 18 • your cards total 14 • Omen is 3. Bind Omen = 17, only 1 away. That is usually stronger than gambling on a blind card.
